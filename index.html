How to Play the Musical Keyboard

1 Finding "Middle C" & Understanding Notes

A piano is organized into repeating patterns of 7 White Keys (C, D, E, F, G, A, B) and 5 Black Keys (grouped in sets of 2 and 3). Look for any group of 2 black keys—the white key immediately to the left is always C!
